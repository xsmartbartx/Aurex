# ADR-0012: Analysis and simulation only

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-15

## Context

Trade execution and personal advice carry licensing obligations outside the project's scope.

## Decision

AUREX does not connect to brokers, place orders or give personalized recommendations. Portfolios are virtual; the AI system prompt forbids recommendations and price targets; public pages and every answer state the boundary.

## Consequences

Scope and legal exposure stay bounded.

## Alternatives considered

Broker integration.
